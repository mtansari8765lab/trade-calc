Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Dr.Building calculators run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit anything you type into a calculator — your measurements never leave your device. There are no accounts and no signup, so we hold no names, email addresses, or passwords. This "we collect nothing" promise covers Dr.Building itself: the third-party services on this site — Google Analytics and Google AdSense — process technical data (such as cookies, device identifiers and IP address) as described below, and our hosting provider necessarily sees standard server-log data.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-39XP28RSP9) to understand aggregate site usage (pages visited, approximate location, device type). Google Analytics uses cookies — such as _ga — to distinguish visits. We do not combine analytics data with anything you type into the calculators, and we do not use analytics data to identify individual visitors.

Like every website, our hosting provider necessarily processes standard web-server log data (such as IP address, requested page, and time) in order to deliver the pages. We do not combine this with anything else and do not use it to identify you.

Your consent choice: when you first visit, a cookie banner asks for your consent. Choosing Accept enables these analytics cookies; choosing Decline keeps them off entirely. Your choice is remembered on your device, and clearing this site's stored data will ask you again. This banner covers analytics consent only — it does not cover advertising consent (see "Advertising consent" below).

Your choices: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and Google offers an opt-out browser add-on for Google Analytics. Blocking cookies does not affect the calculators — they work fully without them.

Data retention

Because calculator inputs never leave your browser, there is nothing to retain from your use of the tools themselves. Aggregate analytics data is held by Google under its own retention settings, and advertising cookie data is governed by Google's policies linked in the Advertising section below. We keep no user database, no logs of calculator inputs, and no profiles — there is simply nothing of yours on our servers to retain or delete.

Advertising

Dr.Building is approved for Google AdSense and uses Google advertising services, including Auto Ads, to serve ads that keep the calculators free. Ads may not appear on every page or every visit.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie), device identifiers, IP address and similar technologies to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see the same ad, and help prevent invalid ad activity. Whether you see personalized or non-personalized ads depends on your location and your consent choices. You can control ad personalization at Google's Ads Settings. See Google's advertising information and Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising consent — current gap

The cookie banner on this site controls analytics consent only: it sets analytics_storage and does not set advertising-consent signals (ad_storage or ad_personalization). No Google-certified consent management platform (CMP), such as Google Funding Choices, is installed on this site, and no advertising-consent choice is collected or recorded here. This banner therefore does not, by itself, satisfy consent requirements for personalized advertising in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. Until a certified CMP is added, advertising consent for visitors in those regions is not collected through this site. This is a known gap we disclose here rather than claim otherwise.

Children's privacy

Dr.Building is a general-audience site not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the calculators run entirely in your browser, there is nothing for a child (or anyone) to submit to us in the first place. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it, and material changes will be described here. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
