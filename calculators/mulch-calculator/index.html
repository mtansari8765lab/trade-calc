About this mulch calculator

The mulch question is always the same: area × depth. A 20×4 ft bed at 3 inches deep needs 20 cubic feet — about three-quarters of a cubic yard, or 10 two-cubic-foot bags. The calculator handles the unit conversions and tells you both the bulk yardage and the bag count, so you can price both options.

Three inches is the sweet spot for weed suppression and moisture retention. Less than 2 inches and weeds punch through; more than 4 inches and you risk suffocating shallow roots and inviting voles. Top up annually — mulch decomposes, which is the point, but it means last year's 3 inches is this year's 1.5.

The bulk-vs-bag decision is pure economics. Bagged mulch runs 2–3× the per-yard price of bulk, but bulk has delivery minimums (often 2–3 yards) and needs a driveway spot for the pile. Under ~2 yards, bags usually win on total cost and convenience.

How to use

  1. Measure each bed's length and width in feet (irregular beds: measure the bounding rectangle and shave 10–20%).
  2. Enter your target depth in inches — 3 is standard.
  3. Press Calculate for cubic yards and bag count.
  4. Price bulk vs. bags: bulk wins above ~2 cubic yards.

Good to know

  • Assumes rectangular beds; irregular shapes need the bounding-box adjustment noted above.
  • Bag size assumed at 2 cu ft — check your product (1.5 and 3 cu ft bags exist).
  • Same math works for topsoil and compost — but soil compacts, so add ~10% for soil beds.

Frequently asked questions

How much mulch do I need for a 10x10 area?

At 3 inches deep: 10 × 10 × 0.25 = 25 cu ft ≈ 0.93 cubic yards, or 13 two-cu-ft bags.

How many bags of mulch are in a cubic yard?

About 13.5 two-cubic-foot bags per cubic yard (27 ÷ 2).

How deep should mulch be?

2–4 inches, with 3 inches the standard recommendation. Keep mulch a few inches away from trunks and stems.

Is it cheaper to buy mulch in bulk or bags?

Bulk is much cheaper per yard — but delivery minimums and the pile location matter. Under ~2 yards, bags are usually the better deal all-in.

How do you calculate mulch?

Length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in ÷ 12) = cubic feet; divide by 27 for cubic yards, or by 2 for 2-cu-ft bags.
