About this roof pitch calculator

Roof pitch is written as x:12 — the vertical rise in inches for every 12 inches of horizontal run. A 6:12 roof rises 6 inches for each foot you move across, which is an angle of 26.57 degrees, a 50% slope, and a pitch multiplier of 1.118. The three forms are the same roof described three ways: pitch = rise ÷ run × 12, angle = atan(rise ÷ run), and slope % = rise ÷ run × 100. This calculator converts between them, so you can start from whichever number you actually have — a tape measurement, a figure on the plans, or an angle-finder reading.

To measure pitch on an existing roof, hold a level 12 inches out from the roof surface and measure straight down from the level to the roof — that drop is the rise for a 12-inch run. From inside the attic, measure the vertical rise from the wall plate to the ridge and the horizontal run from the plate to the ridge centerline, then divide. A speed square laid on a rafter reads the pitch directly, and a digital angle finder gives the angle, which this calculator converts back to x:12. Whatever you measure, rise and run must use the same unit.

The pitch multiplier (also called the slope factor or rafter-length factor) is √(1 + (rise ÷ run)²) — the length along the slope for each unit of horizontal run, straight from the Pythagorean theorem. Multiply any horizontal run by it for the rafter length, or any footprint area by it for the roof area: a 6:12 roof has 11.8% more surface than its footprint. The multiplier is also what our roofing shingle calculator and roof replacement cost estimator use to turn your footprint into squares. Worked example: a 28-ft span at 6:12 with a 12-inch overhang gives a 15-ft run (14 ft half-span + 1 ft overhang) × 1.118 = 16.77 ft of common rafter, a ridge 7 ft above the wall plate, and — on a 40-ft-long building — 1,342 sq ft of gable roof area.

Two code numbers are worth knowing before you order materials. Asphalt shingles need a minimum slope of 2:12, and between 2:12 and 4:12 they need two layers of underlayment (IRC R905.2.2) — this calculator flags both cases in the result. The common residential range is 4:12 to 9:12; 6:12 is the most common single pitch. Below 4:12 a roof is low-slope, above 9:12 it is steep enough to change how crews work and what the job costs.

How to use

  1. Choose how you want to enter the pitch: rise and run, x:12, or angle in degrees — then fill in only the fields for that mode.
  2. Optionally add the building span (wall to wall) and eave overhang for rafter length and ridge height, plus the building length for roof area.
  3. Press Calculate for pitch, angle, slope %, multiplier, rafter length, and roof area.
  4. Use the pitch and roof area in our roofing shingle calculator to price the job.

Good to know

  • Geometry only — this calculator does not size rafters. Rafter size depends on span, spacing, lumber species, snow load, and wind; span tables and local code govern sizing.
  • Assumes a simple gable roof. Hip roofs, valleys, and dormers should be measured in sections and added.
  • Rafter length is a line length along the slope: it does not deduct half the ridge board, and it does not allow for plumb cuts, seat cuts, or the birdsmouth.
  • Roof area is footprint × multiplier (plus overhang when entered). It does not add waste — the shingle calculator adds that.

Formula & standards

Formula: Pitch x:12 = rise ÷ run × 12; angle = atan(rise ÷ run); slope % = rise ÷ run × 100; pitch multiplier = √(1 + (rise ÷ run)²); rafter length = run × multiplier.

Standard: Asphalt shingles need a minimum 2:12 slope, with two layers of underlayment below 4:12 (IRC R905.2.2).

Waste allowance: No waste field on this calculator — rafter length is a line length; add overhang and a cutting allowance separately.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate roof pitch from rise and run?

Divide the rise by the run and multiply by 12: pitch = rise ÷ run × 12. A 6-inch rise over a 12-inch run is 6:12; a 7-inch rise over a 14-inch run is also 6:12, because the ratio is what matters.

How do I convert roof pitch to degrees?

Angle = atan(pitch ÷ 12). A 6:12 pitch is atan(0.5) = 26.57 degrees; 4:12 is 18.43 degrees; 12:12 is 45 degrees. Enter the angle above and the calculator converts it back to x:12.

What is the pitch multiplier (rafter factor)?

The length along the slope for each unit of horizontal run: √(1 + (rise ÷ run)²). At 6:12 it is 1.118, at 12:12 it is 1.414. Multiply a run by it for rafter length, or a footprint area by it for roof area.

How long is a common rafter?

Common rafter length = (half the span + overhang) × pitch multiplier. For a 28-ft span at 6:12 with a 12-inch overhang: (14 + 1) × 1.118 = 16.77 ft. That is a line length — it does not deduct half the ridge board or allow for cuts.

What is the minimum roof pitch for asphalt shingles?

2:12, per IRC R905.2.2. Between 2:12 and 4:12, shingles need two layers of underlayment. Below 2:12, use a low-slope covering such as membrane roofing instead of shingles.

How do I measure roof pitch on an existing roof?

Hold a level 12 inches out from the roof surface and measure straight down from the level to the roof — that drop in inches is the pitch (a 6-inch drop is 6:12). From the attic, measure the vertical rise and horizontal run and divide. A speed square on a rafter or a digital angle finder works too.
