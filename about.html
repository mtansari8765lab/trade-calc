About TradeCalc

TradeCalc is a free collection of calculators for home improvement and trades work — concrete volumes, lumber board feet, roofing squares, paint coverage, fence materials, landscaping quantities, and project cost estimators. It exists for one reason: job-site math should take ten seconds, not ten minutes with a phone calculator and a scrap of paper.

Every calculator runs 100% in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere — there is no account, no signup, and no server processing your numbers. Cost estimators are planning tools with clearly labeled assumptions; they are not contractor quotes, and you should always get local quotes before committing to a budget.

TradeCalc is an independent project. It is not affiliated with any manufacturer, retailer, or contractor network mentioned in guides or examples.
