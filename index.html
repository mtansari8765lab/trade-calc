Why TradeCalc?

Home improvement runs on arithmetic: how many bags of concrete, how many shingles, how many fence pickets — and what it will all cost. Getting the math wrong means a stalled project or wasted money. TradeCalc gives you job-site calculators for the measurements tradespeople and DIYers actually need, with waste allowances and cost ranges built in.

Every calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your measurements are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere. Cost estimators are clearly labeled as planning estimates, not contractor quotes — always get local quotes before you budget.
